Owner's Guide

Setup & Usage Instructions

Thank you for purchasing PROPR. Follow these steps to install or remove your inboard propeller safely.

1

Identify Your Shaft Size

Confirm your propeller shaft diameter before use. PROPR tools are sized to specific shafts — using the wrong size can result in improper threading. Your shaft diameter is stamped on the tool packaging and engraved on the disc face. Standard SAE sizes range from 1¾″ to 6½″.

2

Clean the Shaft Taper

Remove any marine growth, corrosion, or old sealant from both the shaft taper and the propeller hub bore. A clean, dry contact surface ensures the propeller seats evenly and reduces the force required for installation.

3

Thread the PROPR Disc onto the Shaft

With the propeller loosely positioned on the taper, thread the PROPR disc onto the shaft end by hand until it contacts the hub. Make sure the disc is aligned straight and the threads are engaged cleanly — do not cross-thread.

Never use power tools to thread the disc. Hand-tighten only before applying the wrench.
4

Tighten to Press the Propeller On

Using a standard wrench on the disc's hex flats, tighten the PROPR to press the propeller firmly onto the shaft taper. The force is applied directly in line with the shaft — no hammering, no blocking blades, and no side-loading. Continue tightening until the propeller is fully seated and secure.

5

Removal — Reverse the Process

To pull the propeller, reverse the disc on the shaft so that tightening pushes against the hub in the opposite direction. As you turn the wrench, PROPR exerts up to 42 tons of force directly down the taper, breaking the propeller free without any blade contact.

6

Storage

After use, wipe the disc clean and store it in the included hard case. The compact design fits easily in an engine room or tool locker. Rinse with fresh water after saltwater use to prevent surface corrosion.
