Refund Policy

If you are not completely satisfied with your PROPR tool, you may request a return within 30 days of delivery. The tool must be in its original condition, unused, and returned in the original packaging. Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to a manufacturing defect or shipping error on our part.

To initiate a return, please contact us using the form above with your order number and a brief description of the reason. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of receiving the returned item. Custom-order or specially threaded units are non-refundable. Please reach out with any questions — we're here to help.

Coverage

Warranty

Every PROPR tool is covered by a manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions. This warranty covers the structural integrity of the disc, threading, and machined surfaces. It does not cover damage resulting from misuse, modification, or use with incompatible shaft sizes. To submit a warranty claim, contact our team with your order number, photographs of the issue, and a description of how the tool was being used. We will evaluate each claim individually and provide a replacement or repair at our discretion.
