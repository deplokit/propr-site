PROPR is a precision-machined propeller installation and removal tool designed for inboard shafts. Unlike traditional methods that rely on hammers, pry bars, or blade-contact pullers, PROPR presses the hub directly in line with the shaft — the mechanically correct approach. This eliminates the risk of propeller loosening on the taper, which can lead to vibration, keyway cracks, and eventual shaft failure. Used and trusted by leading marine builders and service yards worldwide.
